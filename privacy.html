Privacy policy

Who uses it: only the owner of this app, reading their own account. No other users.

What it reads: read-only access to the owner's sleep, activity and fitness, health metrics, and profile data through the Google Health API.

Where data goes: it is stored only on the owner's personal computer. It is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API data: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
